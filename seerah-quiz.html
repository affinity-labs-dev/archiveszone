What this Seerah quiz covers

The questions follow the life of the Prophet ﷺ in order, so playing through builds a timeline rather than a pile of trivia.

🏢

Birth & early life

Mecca and the Quraysh, his parents Abdullah and Aminah, and the grandfather and uncle who raised him.

🔭

The first revelation

The Cave of Hira, the Angel Jibril (AS), and the beginning of prophethood at around age forty.

👣

The Hijra

The Cave of Thawr, the migration to Medina in 622 CE, and the first mosque at Quba.

⚽

Badr, Uhud & Khandaq

The major battles of the Medinan years and the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah.

🕌

The return to Mecca

The peaceful entry into Mecca in 630 CE and the years that followed.

📜

The Farewell Sermon

The final pilgrimage at Arafat, and his passing in Medina in 632 CE.

Seerah quiz questions and answers

A sample of the Seerah questions with their answers, for anyone who would rather read than play.

  1. In which city was Prophet Muhammad ﷺ born?Mecca. The Prophet ﷺ was born in Mecca around 570 CE.
  2. What was the name of the Prophet's ﷺ father?Abdullah. His father, Abdullah, died before he was born.
  3. What was the name of the Prophet's ﷺ mother?Aminah. His mother was Aminah bint Wahb.
  4. Who was the first wife of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ?Khadijah (RA). Khadijah bint Khuwaylid (RA) was his first wife and the first person to accept Islam.
  5. Which angel brought revelation to the Prophet ﷺ?Jibril (AS). The Angel Jibril (Gabriel) (AS) conveyed the revelation.
  6. To which city did the Prophet ﷺ migrate from Mecca?Medina. He migrated to Medina in 622 CE, in the event called the Hijra.
  7. Which uncle raised the Prophet ﷺ after his grandfather died?Abu Talib. His uncle Abu Talib became his guardian and protector.
  8. What is the migration from Mecca to Medina called?Hijra. It is called the Hijra, and it marks year one of the Islamic calendar.
  9. In which year CE did the Hijra take place?622 CE. The Hijra took place in 622 CE.
  10. Who was the first muezzin, the first to call the adhan?Bilal ibn Rabah (RA). Bilal ibn Rabah (RA), a freed Abyssinian slave, was the first muezzin.
  11. What was the first major battle fought by the Muslims of Medina?Badr. The Battle of Badr took place in 624 CE.
  12. Which battle followed Badr, in which the Muslims suffered heavy losses?Uhud. The Battle of Uhud was fought in 625 CE.

Frequently asked questions about the Seerah

What does 'Seerah' mean?

Seerah refers to the biography of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ: his life, character and the events from his birth in Mecca to his passing in Medina.

Where was Prophet Muhammad ﷺ born?

He was born in Mecca around 570 CE, into the tribe of Quraysh. His father Abdullah died before his birth and his mother Aminah died when he was young.

At what age did the Prophet ﷺ receive the first revelation?

At about 40, in the Cave of Hira near Mecca, when the Angel Jibril (AS) brought the first verses of the Quran.

What is the Hijra?

The Hijra is the migration from Mecca to Medina in 622 CE. It marks year one of the Islamic (Hijri) calendar.
