Every kids question, with the answer

All 30 questions written out with their answers, for reading aloud away from a screen: the dinner table, the school run, a madrasah class or a Ramadan quiz night.

  1. What is the holy book of Islam called?The Quran. The Quran is the holy book of Islam, believed by Muslims to be the word of God.
  2. What is the first surah of the Quran?Al-Fatihah. Al-Fatihah, 'The Opening', is the first surah and is recited in every prayer.
  3. In which language was the Quran revealed?Arabic. The Quran was revealed in Arabic.
  4. In which month was the Quran first revealed?Ramadan. The Quran says it was sent down in the month of Ramadan.
  5. What is a 'surah'?A chapter of the Quran. A surah is a chapter of the Quran. There are 114 in total.
  6. What is an 'ayah'?A verse. An ayah is a verse of the Quran.
  7. To whom was the Quran revealed?Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. The Quran was revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ over roughly 23 years.
  8. In which city was Prophet Muhammad ﷺ born?Mecca. The Prophet ﷺ was born in Mecca around 570 CE.
  9. What was the name of the Prophet's ﷺ father?Abdullah. His father, Abdullah, died before he was born.
  10. What was the name of the Prophet's ﷺ mother?Aminah. His mother was Aminah bint Wahb.
  11. Who was the first wife of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ?Khadijah (RA). Khadijah bint Khuwaylid (RA) was his first wife and the first person to accept Islam.
  12. Which angel brought revelation to the Prophet ﷺ?Jibril (AS). The Angel Jibril (Gabriel) (AS) conveyed the revelation.
  13. To which city did the Prophet ﷺ migrate from Mecca?Medina. He migrated to Medina in 622 CE, in the event called the Hijra.
  14. Which prophet built an ark to survive a great flood?Nuh (AS). Prophet Nuh (Noah) (AS) built the ark at God's command.
  15. Who is considered the first prophet and the first human?Adam (AS). Adam (AS) is the first human being and the first prophet in Islam.
  16. Which prophet was sent to Pharaoh?Musa (AS). Prophet Musa (Moses) (AS) was sent to Pharaoh in Egypt.
  17. Who was the mother of Prophet Isa (AS)?Maryam (AS). Prophet Isa (Jesus) (AS) was born to Maryam (Mary) (AS).
  18. Which prophet was known for interpreting dreams in Egypt?Yusuf (AS). Prophet Yusuf (Joseph) (AS) interpreted dreams and rose to high office in Egypt.
  19. Which prophet was swallowed by a large fish?Yunus (AS). Prophet Yunus (Jonah) (AS) was swallowed by a great fish and then delivered.
  20. How many pillars of Islam are there?Five. There are five pillars: Shahada, Salah, Zakat, Sawm, and Hajj.
  21. Which pillar means fasting during Ramadan?Sawm. Sawm is fasting from dawn to sunset during Ramadan.
  22. How many obligatory prayers are there each day?Five. Five: Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha.
  23. What is the Shahada?The declaration of faith. The Shahada is the declaration that there is no god but God and Muhammad ﷺ is His messenger.
  24. What does Zakat mean?Giving charity. Zakat is the obligatory giving of a portion of one's wealth to those in need.
  25. What is Hajj?The pilgrimage to Mecca. Hajj is the pilgrimage to Mecca, required once in a lifetime for those able.
  26. What is a hadith?A saying or action of the Prophet ﷺ. A hadith is a recorded saying, action, or approval of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
  27. Which Muslim leader recaptured Jerusalem in 1187 CE?Saladin (Salahuddin). Salahuddin al-Ayyubi, known in the West as Saladin, retook Jerusalem in 1187.
  28. In which city was the famous 'House of Wisdom' located?Baghdad. The House of Wisdom (Bayt al-Hikma) flourished in Abbasid Baghdad.
  29. Which Muslim traveller journeyed roughly 75,000 miles in the 14th century?Ibn Battuta. Ibn Battuta's travels covered much of Africa, the Middle East, and Asia.
  30. Who founded Al-Qarawiyyin, the world's oldest continually operating university?Fatima al-Fihri. Fatima al-Fihri, a Muslim woman, founded it in Fez in 859 CE.

A note for grown-ups

Two honest points. First, what this is not: it does not teach Quran recitation, tajweed, or Arabic, and it is no substitute for a teacher or a maktab. It builds the stories and the context that make those lessons land, and that is all it claims.

Second, how it is paced: rounds are five questions and then they stop, so there is a natural place to put the phone down. Stars are kept in this browser on this device, and no account or sign-up is needed to play.

Questions parents ask

What age is this Islamic quiz for kids?

There are three age bands. Ages 5–7 get the gentlest questions and work best read aloud with you. Ages 8–10 get those plus a wider beginner set. Ages 11+ adds a further layer, including the Seerah and early Islamic history.

Does it show my child the answers?

Yes, immediately. The moment they tap an option the correct answer lights up green and one friendly sentence explains why, so a wrong guess still teaches them something rather than just marking them down.

Can my child play it before they can read well?

Yes, with you alongside them. Read the question and the four options aloud and let them choose. The questions in the youngest band are deliberately short so they are easy to say out loud.

Is it free, and does my child need an account?

It is free to play and no account or sign-up is needed. Their stars are kept in this browser on this device rather than in a profile.

Why stars instead of a score?

Stars are gentle encouragement. Rounds are short and there is nothing to lose, so the focus stays on the story behind each answer rather than on beating a number.

Does it teach Quran reading, tajweed, or Arabic?

No, and we would not claim otherwise. This quiz covers the stories and facts of Islam in plain English. Recitation, tajweed and Arabic need a qualified teacher. This sits alongside that, not instead of it. See online Islamic studies for kids.

Can I use these questions away from a screen?

That is what the printable-style list further down is for: every question with its answer, so you can read them in the car, at the dinner table, or at a madrasah class with no device in sight.
